# 🎬 My Favorite Series: Wonderful Nightmare

## About the Series
**Wonderful Nightmare** is a South Korean fantasy romantic comedy movie released in 2015. It tells the story of a woman who gets a second chance at life and experiences what it is like to live as an ordinary person.

## ⭐ Why I Like It
I like *Wonderful Nightmare* because it combines **comedy, fantasy, and family**. The story is entertaining and also teaches lessons about love, family, and appreciating the people around us.
